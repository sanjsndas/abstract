Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between interior design and interior architecture?

Interior design focuses on finishes, furnishings, and the aesthetic and functional improvement of interior spaces. Interior architecture (sometimes called interior architecture and design) involves structural modifications — moving walls, altering ceiling heights, and designing built-in elements. Many practitioners work across both disciplines.

How do I choose the right lighting for a living room?

Start with ambient light (recessed or ceiling fixtures) for general illumination, then add task lighting (floor lamps, table lamps) for reading, and accent lighting (wall sconces, picture lights) for depth. Aim for a color temperature between 2700K and 3000K for a warm, inviting feel.

What are the most sustainable materials for home renovation?

Reclaimed wood, bamboo, cork, hempcrete, recycled steel, and low-carbon concrete are among the best choices. Also consider local sourcing to reduce transportation emissions and look for materials with third-party certifications like FSC or Cradle to Cradle.

How can I make a small space feel larger?

Use a consistent color palette, maximize natural light, choose furniture with legs (to show floor), use mirrors strategically, and avoid heavy visual clutter. Multi-functional furniture and vertical storage also help.
