Privacy Policy

How we handle your data, respect your privacy, and keep our editorial independence.

Last updated: April 14, 2025 · Effective immediately

At Abstract Built Matter ("we," "our," or "us"), we take your privacy seriously. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website, subscribe to our newsletter, or interact with our content. By using our site, you agree to the practices described here.

In short: We collect only what we need to improve your reading experience, we never sell your data, and we use cookies to understand what resonates with our audience.

1. Information we collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you provide directly: When you fill out our contact form, subscribe to our newsletter, or leave a comment, we may collect your name, email address, and any other details you choose to share.
  • Information collected automatically: Like most websites, we use cookies and similar technologies to collect non-personal information such as your browser type, device, pages visited, and time spent on the site. This helps us understand what content is most useful.
  • Information from third parties: We may receive limited analytics data from services like Google Analytics (anonymized) and social media platforms if you interact with our content there.

2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries, story pitches, or feedback.
  • Send you our newsletter if you have opted in (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • Analyze site traffic and improve our editorial content and user experience.
  • Protect against spam, abuse, and security threats.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Cookies and tracking

We use cookies — small text files stored on your device — to enhance your browsing experience. These include:

  • Essential cookies: Necessary for the site to function (e.g., remembering your cookie preferences).
  • Analytics cookies: We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors navigate our site. The data is aggregated and anonymized.
  • Preference cookies: To remember settings like dark mode or font size (if applicable).

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. For more details, see our Cookies Policy.

4. Third-party services

We use a few trusted third-party services to operate our site and newsletter:

  • Google Analytics — anonymized traffic analysis. Google's privacy policy.
  • Mailchimp (or similar) — for our email newsletter. Your email is stored securely and never shared.
  • Unsplash — some images are loaded from Unsplash's CDN.
  • Cloudflare Fonts / Google Fonts — for typography.

These services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

5. Data retention & security

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law. Newsletter subscriber data is kept until you unsubscribe. Contact form submissions are retained for up to 12 months for reference.

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including SSL encryption and access controls. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location (e.g., under GDPR or CCPA), you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate information.
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your personal data.
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time via the link in the footer.
  • Restriction: Request that we limit how we process your data.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Children's privacy

Our content is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The "last updated" date at the top indicates when revisions were made. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

9. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out:

  • Email: privacy@abstractbuiltmatter.com
  • Postal: Abstract Built Matter, 412 Design District, Studio 7, Brooklyn, NY 11205, USA
  • Or use our contact form.

This policy is provided for informational purposes and does not constitute legal advice. For specific legal concerns, please consult a qualified attorney.
