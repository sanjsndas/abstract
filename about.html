About the Journal

An independent publication exploring the intersection of design, materials, and the spaces we inhabit. We believe good design is a form of quiet activism.

Our Mission

Abstract Built Matter was founded in 2019 with a simple conviction: that the built environment shapes how we live, connect, and care for the planet. We publish in-depth essays, practical guides, and critical perspectives on interior design, architecture, materials, and the art of living well.

In an era of fleeting trends and digital noise, we aim to be a counterpoint — a place for considered writing, rigorous thinking, and visual storytelling that respects the intelligence of our readers.

“We believe good design is a form of quiet activism — it shapes how we live, connect, and care for the planet.” — Lena Ortiz, Editor-in-Chief

Editorial Approach

Every article we publish is grounded in research, observation, and conversations with practitioners. We don't chase fads. We look for ideas that endure — principles of proportion, material honesty, passive design, and spatial psychology that have relevance across cultures and climates.

Our editorial team works with architects, designers, material scientists, and historians to bring depth and nuance to every topic. Whether we're dissecting a joinery detail or examining the carbon footprint of concrete, we aim to inform and provoke in equal measure.

Independence

Abstract Built Matter is entirely independent. We are reader-supported and carry no sponsored content. Our recommendations are based on merit, not advertising relationships. This allows us to speak candidly about the challenges facing our industries — from greenwashing to labor practices — without compromise.

What We Cover

Our editorial spans interior design, architecture, materials, lighting, space planning, furniture, and modern homes. We are particularly interested in the intersection of sustainability and aesthetics — how we can build and live more responsibly without sacrificing beauty or comfort.

We also believe that design is not just for professionals. Our guides are written for anyone who wants to understand their home, their materials, and the forces that shape the built world.
